About the model

Runs use the v3 run-type model: each run is classified (easy, long, tempo, intervals, strides, hills, downhill, trail, race), scored on internal (Banister TRIMP), impact and eccentric load, and stored as a fast metabolic component (τ 0.9 d) plus a slow structural one (τ by muscle × damage class) — Dorn 2012 muscle forces, Vernillo 2017 grade effects, Bontemps 2020 recovery and repeated-bout effect. A thin running base scales structural damage up and slows its clock (Paulsen 2012 unaccustomed-exercise classes), and soreness peaks the next morning rather than at the finish line. Hockey, swimming and gym sessions keep their v2 EMG-based shares (Reilly & Borrie; Pink & Perry). Recovery sessions apply small, evidence-sized reductions (Poppendieck 2016; Bleakley 2012).
